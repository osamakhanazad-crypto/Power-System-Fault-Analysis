# Power System Fault Analysis

## Project Overview
This project presents a fault analysis study of a power system. The objective is to understand the operation of a power system under normal conditions and analyze the effects of a fault on the system.

## Fault Condition Studied
A three-phase symmetrical fault is considered for the analysis. This type of fault can produce very high fault currents and significant voltage drops in the power system.

## Objectives
- Understand basic power system operation.
- Study a three-phase fault condition.
- Analyze the impact of the fault on voltage and current.
- Understand the effects of faults on power system equipment.
- Study the importance of protective relays and circuit breakers.

## System Components
The studied power system consists of:
- Generating station
- Power transformer
- Transmission line
- Load
- Protection system

## Analysis
The fault causes a significant increase in current and a reduction in voltage at the fault location. High fault current can impose thermal and mechanical stresses on electrical equipment. Fast detection and isolation of the faulty section are therefore important for maintaining system reliability and stability.

## Protection
Protective relays and circuit breakers are used to detect and isolate the faulty section of the power system. Proper protection coordination helps minimize equipment damage and power interruptions.

## Report
The detailed fault analysis, observations, calculations, and conclusions are provided in the project report.
